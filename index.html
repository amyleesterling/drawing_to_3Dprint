WHAT THIS VERSION ACTUALLY DOES

No smoke.
A very small mirror.

The browser does deterministic geometry today. Open image-to-3D models can propose richer surfaces tomorrow, but the joint plan and print contract remain ours.

01

Image in

Upload any drawing. Nothing is sent to a server. A draggable body rig keeps the child—not a mystery model—in charge of anatomy.

02

Mechanics in the open

Body plates alternate in depth around pin hinges. Head and all four arms are mechanically attached, not hovering nearby on a spiritual journey.

03

Printable out

The export lays every plate, arm, head, and peg flat on a virtual print bed and produces one STL plus a reproducible JSON recipe.
